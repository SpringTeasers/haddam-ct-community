Haddam, Connecticut

Contact

Haddam has no single switchboard for everything — different services live in different offices. Use the sections below to reach the right place the first time.

Official town business: If your question is about permits, taxes, meetings, records or closures, go straight to the Town of Haddam at haddam.org (opens in a new tab) or call 860-345-8531.

Send a message to this website

This site exists to help residents and visitors find their way around Haddam — what’s here, where to eat and shop, how the town works, and where the official information lives. It is a community project and is not an official Town of Haddam website.

Use this form with questions, corrections or suggestions — for example, if a business listing looks out of date, or if you think a page is missing something useful.

Schools

Haddam’s public schools are part of the regionalized school system shared with Killingworth. For enrollments, calendars and district information, start from the town’s own community pages: haddam.org/31/Community (opens in a new tab).

Community services

Haddam-Killingworth Youth & Family Services (HKYS) serves local youth and families: hkyfs.org (opens in a new tab).

Local history

The Haddam Historical Society welcomes questions about the town’s past: haddamhistory.org (opens in a new tab).

In an emergency

Emergency: Call 911 for any emergency.
