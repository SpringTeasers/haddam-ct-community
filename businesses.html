Local Businesses in Haddam

Haddam’s businesses are small, local and spread across the town’s villages. Every listing on this page is taken from the Town of Haddam’s official online business directory — the names, addresses, phone numbers and descriptions are the town’s own published entries.

The town’s directory currently holds 50 listings across categories that include Farms & Farm Stores, Restaurants & Eateries, Gifts, Hardware, Home Improvement, HVAC Services, Landscaping & Lawn Services, Financial Services, Real Estate, Recreation, Residential Services, Salon / Spa / Beauty, Package Stores, Garden & Plant Nurseries, Heating Oil / Propane / Firewood and Wedding and Event Venues. This page reproduces the listings confirmed from the directory; for the complete, always-current list, use the town’s page.

Own a business in Haddam? The town invites local businesses to add themselves to the directory — see the registration link at the bottom of this page.

List your business in the town directory

If you run a business in Haddam, the Town of Haddam invites you to register it in the official online business directory. The registration form is linked directly from the town’s directory page, and the town also posts a notice about joining the directory in its news feed.

  • Register your business — start at the town’s directory page (opens in a new tab)
  • Town news item: “Join the Haddam Online Business Directory” (opens in a new tab)

A note about this directory

This page reproduces listings from the Town of Haddam’s official online business directory as of October 2026. Businesses open, move and change hands — always confirm details by calling ahead or checking the business’s own website. For the complete and current list of all 50 listings, and to have your own business added, use the town’s directory at haddam.org/BusinessDirectoryii.aspx (opens in a new tab).

If you spot a listing here that is out of date, the fastest fix is to keep the town’s directory current — that is the source this page draws from.
