Haddam, Connecticut

Things to Do in Haddam

Haddam’s best attractions are outdoors and local: a river that runs through the middle of town, a state forest, a town cove on the water, a working village center, and a library that runs programs year-round for every age.

  • Water & woods
  • Library programs
  • Town events
  • Villages & dining

Below are places and pastimes confirmed by the town, the library or the businesses themselves.

Town parks & open space

The town maintains acres of recreational open space, managed with public input through the Park & Recreation Commission.

River & waterfront

Water on both sides of town, including Higganum Cove and the Connecticut River itself, the town’s defining feature for more than 350 years.

Parks, trails & forest

Seven Falls State Park beside Higganum village, and part of Cockaponset State Forest within the town’s borders.

Seasonal events

Fall Fest and Trunk or Treat, the Pumpkin Run, school fundraisers and tours of Haddam’s historic jail.

On the water and in the woods

Higganum Cove

A town waterfront area in Higganum, cared for with the involvement of the Higganum Cove Advisory Committee. The town publishes a resident parking notice for the cove, so if you are visiting from out of town, check the town’s current parking rules before you go.

Town of Haddam — news: “Parking at Higganum Cove for Town Residents” (opens in a new tab)

Seven Falls State Park

A state park right beside the Higganum village center — the neighboring business on Saybrook Road describes itself as “right next door to 7 Falls State Park.” It pairs naturally with breakfast or lunch in the village.

Town of Haddam business directory (Debs Restaurant listing) (opens in a new tab)

Cockaponset State Forest

Part of Cockaponset State Forest lies within Haddam, making the town one of the gateways to one of Connecticut’s large state forests.

The Connecticut River

Haddam is the only town in Connecticut bisected by the Connecticut River, so there is water on both sides of town — the river has been the town’s defining feature for more than 350 years, as a route, a livelihood and now a place for recreation.

The town maintains acres of recreational open space, and the Park & Recreation Commission meets regularly on the town calendar — a good place to find out about fields, programs and facilities.

Town events and seasonal traditions

Haddam’s calendar mixes civic business with community celebrations. Items seen posted on the town’s own website during the fall season give a good picture of the rhythm of the year.

Autumn — celebrations and fundraisers
  • Fall Fest and Trunk or Treat — a town autumn celebration
  • Pumpkin Run — a seasonal town event
  • HKHS Boys Soccer Team Pumpkin, Mum & Bake Sale — a school sports fundraiser in October
  • Columbus Day — town offices and the town garage close for the holiday
History — tours and the town jail
  • Cell Block Tales: Tour the Jail and Meet the Prisoners — a program built around Haddam’s historic jail, which served the county until the early 1970s
  • Haddam Historical Society — the town’s historical society, which welcomes questions about Haddam’s past
Public meetings — open to residents
  • Haddam Volunteer Fire Company Auxiliary meetings
  • Higganum Cove Advisory Committee and Economic Development Commission meetings — open to the public, and useful if you want to be part of what happens next in town

Because these dates change every year, always check the live town calendar before you plan around an event.

Eat, shop and wander the villages

Haddam’s commercial life is concentrated in its villages, above all Higganum and Tylerville, which the town describes as remaining viable commercial centers. A morning in Higganum can look like breakfast at a village restaurant, a walk by the cove, and a stop at the country store near the old railroad station site.
