Sample section — please read: This is a sample news and events section, built to show how the page will look. The items below were real when this site was written (October 2026), but they are not updated automatically and this is not an official Town of Haddam page. For current news, meeting dates and closures, always use the official sources: town news (opens in a new tab), town calendar (opens in a new tab), and library events (opens in a new tab).

The three places to look first

  1. Town of Haddam news and alerts — official announcements, closures and town notices: haddam.org/CivicAlerts.aspx (opens in a new tab)
  2. Town of Haddam calendar — every posted board, commission and town event date: haddam.org/Calendar.aspx (opens in a new tab)
  3. Brainerd Memorial Library events — programs for all ages, with registration: library event calendar (opens in a new tab)

If you only do one thing, sign up for the town’s Everbridge emergency notifications so alerts reach you directly: emergency notification sign-up (opens in a new tab).

Town news

New food scrap drop-off site opens at 11 Jail Hill Road

A new food-scrap drop-off site opened October 1, 2026, in a shed at 11 Jail Hill Road, near the Haddam Social Services office. The site gives residents an easy way to keep food waste out of the regular trash stream.

Source: Town of Haddam news item (opens in a new tab)

Closure

Town Office Building and Town Garage closed for Columbus Day

The town posted a holiday closure notice for the Town Office Building and Town Garage. Town holiday closures are announced in the news feed — check it before you make a trip to Town Hall.

Source: Town of Haddam news item (opens in a new tab)

Emergency alerts

Sign up for emergency notifications

Haddam sends emergency alerts through Everbridge. Residents can sign up through the town website so that alerts arrive directly rather than being discovered later.

Source: Town of Haddam news item (opens in a new tab)

Business

Join the Haddam online business directory

The town invites Haddam businesses to add themselves to the official online business directory — the same source this site’s Local Businesses page draws from.

Source: Town of Haddam news item (opens in a new tab)

Higganum Cove

Parking at Higganum Cove for town residents

The town published details about resident parking at Higganum Cove. If you are visiting the cove from out of town, check the current rules before you go.

Source: Town of Haddam news item (opens in a new tab)

Update

First Selectman’s monthly update

Haddam Events Magazine

The Haddam Events Magazine is the Town of Haddam’s quarterly publication, serving Haddam, Higganum and Haddam Neck. It is mailed to homes and also published online, and it remains the best single round-up of town news and events.

  • Current and back issues of Haddam Events Magazine (opens in a new tab)
