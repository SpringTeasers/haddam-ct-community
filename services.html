News, alerts and staying informed

Emergency: Haddam sends emergency alerts through Everbridge. Sign up through the town’s website so you receive alerts directly. Details: haddam.org — emergency notification sign-up (opens in a new tab).

  • Town news and announcements (opens in a new tab)
  • Town calendar — meetings and events (opens in a new tab)
  • Agendas and minutes (opens in a new tab)
  • Haddam Events Magazine (opens in a new tab)
  • Haddam Events Magazine archive (opens in a new tab)
  • Town Facebook page (opens in a new tab)
  • MyAccount — choose which notifications you receive (opens in a new tab)

Haddam Events Magazine is the town’s quarterly publication serving Haddam, Higganum and Haddam Neck. It is mailed to homes and also available online, which makes it a good way to catch up if you missed a mailing.

MyAccount lets residents create an account to choose which notifications they receive and to save form progress.

Brainerd Memorial Library

Address
920 Saybrook Road, Haddam, CT 06438
Phone
860-345-2204

The library offers book clubs, knitting, puzzle and culinary clubs, a French conversation club, museum passes, a games and puzzles collection, job-search resources, children’s and teen programs, genealogy resources and The Book Nook, its onsite used bookstore. It also hosts community talks — including presentations by the Haddam Land Trust on the town’s preserves.

Note: Hours change seasonally — check the library’s own site for current opening hours.

Schools

Haddam’s public schools are part of the regionalized school system shared with the neighboring town of Killingworth. The town’s calendar carries school and school-sports events, and the school district publishes its own information for families.

For enrollments, calendars, bus routes and district news, use the town’s site and the district’s own pages rather than relying on a secondhand summary:

  • Town of Haddam — schools and community (opens in a new tab)
  • Town calendar, which includes school events (opens in a new tab)

Note: This page deliberately does not publish a school address or district phone number — none was confirmed against a primary source. Use the district’s own website for those details.
